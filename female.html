Women Health Q & A

What diet is best for women?

A balanced diet with iron-rich foods, calcium, protein, fruits, vegetables, and enough water helps maintain overall health.

How to manage menstrual pain naturally?

Light exercise, warm compress, hydration, and proper rest can help reduce menstrual discomfort.

How often should women get health checkups?

Regular checkups every 6–12 months are recommended, including blood tests and gynecological consultations.

What precautions are needed during pregnancy?

Proper nutrition, regular doctor visits, avoiding alcohol/smoking, and stress management are very important.

How to improve mental wellbeing?

Adequate sleep, mindfulness, talking to trusted people, and seeking professional help when needed.
