Common Questions

How much should my baby sleep?

Newborns sleep 14–17 hours a day.

Is fever after vaccination normal?

Yes, mild fever is common. Consult a doctor if severe.

Can I give medicine without prescription?

No. Always consult a pediatrician.

Emergency Helpline

🚑 Ambulance: 108 👶 Child Helpline: 1098

If baby has breathing trouble, seizures, or continuous crying – seek help immediately.
